Understand your Spotify taste

Upload the files Spotify already lets you download, and scroll through your music from four angles: song details, genre mix, how a second playlist compares, and your listening habits over time. Everything is parsed right here in your browser; nothing you upload is ever sent to a server.

Upload your playlist

Everything below unlocks once you upload a playlist export from Exportify — the same file Song Analysis, Genre, and Compare use.

  1. Go to exportify.net and sign in with your Spotify account. Exportify will ask you to link your account and agree to Spotify's terms.
  2. Open one of your playlists and click Export. The CSV downloads immediately, with every column — including audio features and genres — included automatically. There are no export settings to configure.
  3. Drop the CSV below, or click to browse. You can select more than one playlist at once.

      Upload your streaming history

      See when you actually listen: hours streamed by year and month, a day-of-week × time-of-day heatmap, and your top artists and songs. This needs a separate file from Exportify: Spotify's Extended Streaming History, which takes longer to arrive.

      1. Go to Spotify's privacy page and sign in to your account.
      2. Scroll to Download your data and tick the box for Extended streaming history specifically. It's a separate option from the basic account data, so make sure that one is checked before you submit the request. Click the Request Data button.
      3. Wait for Spotify's email. Unlike Exportify, this isn't instant: it usually takes a few days, and can take up to about a month.
      4. When the email arrives, download and unzip my_spotify_data.zip.
      5. Drop every Streaming_History_Audio_*.json file below at once (video history files are welcome too).

          About this project

          Team

          • Prithvi Kanherkar
          • Sabrina Lam

          Goals

          Spotify Wrapped gives every listener one polished snapshot a year. This project lets you dig in any time, on your own terms: it breaks down a playlist's audio-feature makeup (danceability, energy, tempo, and more) and genre mix, and turns your Extended Streaming History into a picture of your actual listening habits: which months and times of day you listen most, and which artists and songs you keep coming back to. The goal is simply to give Spotify listeners more insight into their own listening trends than the Spotify app itself surfaces.

          Intended audience

          The Spotify userbase at large: any listener curious about their own habits, not just data analysts. No technical background is required: each page walks you through downloading and uploading the right file, and the charts take it from there.

          Data sources

          Exportify
          A third-party tool that pulls a playlist's track data, audio features, and genres from the Spotify Web API and exports it as a CSV. Powers Song Analysis and Genre.
          Spotify's data download tool
          Spotify's own account privacy page, where you can request your Extended Streaming History: a full record of what you've played and when. Powers Streaming Trends.

          Both files are processed entirely in your browser; nothing you upload is ever sent to a server.
