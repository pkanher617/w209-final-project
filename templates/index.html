Song Analysis

Browse every track in a playlist: see your top songs, search for any track, and compare its full audio-feature profile (danceability, energy, tempo, and more) against your library's average. This page and Genre both run on the same file, from Exportify. Everything is parsed locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

  1. Go to exportify.net and sign in with your Spotify account — Exportify will ask you to link your account and agree to Spotify's terms.
  2. Open one of your playlists, then in Exportify's export settings make sure audio features and genres are turned on.
  3. Click Export — the CSV downloads immediately, no waiting required.
  4. Drop the CSV below, or click to browse. You can select more than one playlist at once.

      Streaming Trends

      See when you actually listen: hours streamed by year and month, a day-of-week × time-of-day heatmap, and your top artists and songs. This page runs on Spotify's Extended Streaming History — a different, separate file from Exportify that takes longer to arrive. Files are parsed locally and never uploaded.

      1. Go to Spotify's privacy page and sign in to your account.
      2. Scroll to Download your data and tick the box for Extended streaming history specifically — it's a separate option from the basic account data, so make sure that one is checked before you submit the request.
      3. Wait for Spotify's email. Unlike Exportify, this isn't instant: it usually takes a few days, and can take up to about a month.
      4. When the email arrives, download and unzip my_spotify_data.zip.
      5. Drop every Streaming_History_Audio_*.json file below at once (video history files are welcome too).
